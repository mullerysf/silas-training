🌿 Stephanie's Training

The Plan

4 days/week, home gym (dumbbells + bands). Built around progressive overload on the lifts, with a short metabolic finisher each session to help the fat-loss side without turning every day into pure cardio.

Weekly Split

Day 1 — Upper A (Push Focus)
Day 2 — Lower A (Squat Focus)
Day 3 — Rest or light walk
Day 4 — Upper B (Pull Focus)
Day 5 — Lower B (Hinge/Glute Focus)
Day 6-7 — Rest / optional walk

How to progress

Same weight feels easy for all reps two sessions in a row → go up one increment next time (2.5–5 lb dumbbells, or one band level). Track everything in the Workouts tab so you're not guessing week to week.

Quick Targets

Daily Anchors

Protein at every meal (not just dinner) matters more than any single number. Aim for 3–4 protein-containing meals/snacks spread through the day.

The honest version

Fat loss + muscle gain at the same time ("recomposition") works best with: a moderate calorie deficit (roughly 300–500 cal/day below maintenance — not more), protein high enough to protect muscle, and consistent progressive lifting. Big deficits or skipping meals tend to backfire — you lose muscle along with fat and it's harder to sustain. This app won't push you toward extreme numbers on purpose.
